Health Tips

Simple tips to help you and your family stay healthy.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 2–3 litres of water every day to stay healthy.

Healthy Diet

Eat fresh fruits, vegetables and balanced meals every day.

Exercise

Walk or exercise for at least 30 minutes daily.

Proper Sleep

Adults should sleep 7–8 hours every night.

Take Medicines Correctly

Always take medicines exactly as prescribed by your doctor.

Boost Immunity

Eat nutritious food and maintain good hygiene to strengthen immunity.

Regular Health Check-ups

Monitor blood pressure, sugar levels and overall health regularly.

Hand Hygiene

Wash your hands frequently to prevent infections.

Medical Emergency?

Visit the nearest hospital or contact emergency medical services immediately.
